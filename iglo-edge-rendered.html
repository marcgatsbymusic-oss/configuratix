IGLO EDGE

Our new, most technologically advanced window is distinguished by an excellent thermal insulation parameter of Uw = 0.66 W/(m²K)* and a modern, angular profile shape. The extremely good thermal insulation parameters are due, among other things, to the 7-chamber profile design and 3 EPDM gaskets, including the central gasket.

  • Double-chamber glazing package Ug = 0.5 W/(m²K)
  • Swisspacer Ultimate plastic warm frame
  • V-perfect weld
  • 4 anti-theft strikers according to the size of the sash and the hardware system
  • Microventilation
  • DUBLIN aluminium window handle
  • Handle misplacement locking mechanism
  • Perimeter, glazing and central gaskets in black or grey
  • Filling of the lower hardware groove
  • Sill trim
  • Wide selection of PVC veneer colours
